ShopTriage architecture: v1 vs. v2
v1 was an earlier headless call-triage project. Everything outlined in blue is new in ShopTriage (v2).
Same as v1New in v2
v1: Property Management Call Triage (headless)

↓
S3: recordings/ (existing bucket, left untouched)
↓
Lambda: start_transcription
↓
Amazon Transcribe
↓
EventBridge: Transcribe job state change
↓
Lambda: process_transcript (also saves a plain-text copy to an archive bucket)
↓
Claude: category, urgency flags, summary, action items
↓
DynamoDB: CallLog (one item per call, Scan-based reads)
Amazon SES: per-call triage email to the property manager
Weekly digest
EventBridge: weekly schedule (Monday 08:00)
↓
Lambda: weekly_summary (scans the past 7 days of CallLog; Claude writes the highlights)
↓
Amazon SES: weekly digest email
